## ✨ Overview

**Se7enX Player** is a sleek, browser-based local music player that gives you full control over your audio files — no internet, no tracking, no subscriptions. Just your music, beautifully presented.

Click a track and it starts automatically. The play button and active playlist item stay animated while music is playing, and the local-files screen gets a gentle color motion for a premium feel.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎧 **Instant Playback** — Click a track and it plays immediately, no extra steps
- 📂 **Drag & Drop Support** — Drop audio files directly into the player
- 🎼 **Multi-Format Support** — MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC
- 🎛️ **Built-in 5-Band EQ** — 60Hz · 170Hz · 350Hz · 1000Hz · 3500Hz
- 🔊 **Volume Control** — Smooth slider with percentage display
- 📋 **Smart Playlist** — Thumbnail, track name, artist, play & delete per track
- 🔍 **Search Local Tracks** — Instantly filter your playlist
- 🌀 **Animated UI** — Spinning vinyl disc and animated play button while playing
- 🌈 **Ambient Background** — Gentle color motion on the player screen
- 💻 **100% Local** — No data leaves your device. Ever.

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. **Open** the app in your browser
2. **Drop files** onto the player or click **"Choose files"**
3. **Click any track** in the playlist to start playback
4. **Adjust EQ** sliders for your preferred sound profile
5. **Search** your playlist using the search bar
